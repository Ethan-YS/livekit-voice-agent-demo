# Procedures

> Demo content. Fictional procedures.

## Escalating an urgent ticket
If an urgent ticket has no technician assigned after 1 hour, the dispatch desk calls the depot lead. If it is still unassigned after 2 hours, the operations manager is told and may approve overtime or a subcontractor.

## Refrigerant leaks
Only technicians with an up-to-date refrigerant handling certificate may work on refrigerant leaks. Leaks over 5 kilograms must be logged in the compliance register on the same day.

## Safety stand-down
Any technician can stop a job if it is unsafe. They report it to the depot lead, and the job is rescheduled with a second technician.

## Customer complaints
Complaints are logged as a high priority ticket and owned by a depot lead. The customer gets a call back within one business day.
