# Policies

> Demo content. Fictional policies.

## Refunds and cancellations
Monthly maintenance plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the current month. Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 60 days, minus any inspections already done. After 60 days, annual plans are not refundable but can be transferred to another site.

## Repair warranty
All repairs carry a 90 day labour warranty. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, usually one year. A repeat fault on the same unit within 90 days is fixed free of charge.

## Response times (SLA)
Urgent tickets (no cooling or heating at a clinic, food business or for vulnerable residents): technician on site within 4 hours. High: next business day. Normal: within 3 business days. Low: within 10 business days.

## Billing disputes
Billing disputes are handled by the billing team within 5 business days. The disputed amount is put on hold while it is reviewed, and the customer is not chased for it.
