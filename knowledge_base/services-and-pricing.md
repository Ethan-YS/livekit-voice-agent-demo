# Services and pricing

> Demo content. Fictional prices.

## Standard callout
A standard callout costs 95 dollars and includes the first 30 minutes on site. After that, labour is billed at 120 dollars per hour in 15 minute steps. Parts are billed at list price.

## Emergency callout
Emergency callouts (within 4 hours, any time of day) cost 220 dollars including the first hour on site. Nights, Sundays and public holidays add a 50 percent surcharge on labour.

## Maintenance plans
The Basic plan is 39 dollars per unit per month and covers two scheduled inspections a year. The Plus plan is 69 dollars per unit per month and adds priority response and 15 percent off parts. Plans are billed monthly or annually; annual billing gets two months free.

## Quotes for new equipment
Quotes for new heat pumps, rooftop units and walk-in coolers are free. A senior technician visits the site, and the written quote is sent within 3 business days.
